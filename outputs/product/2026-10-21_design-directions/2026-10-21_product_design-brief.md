# WaveWatch design brief, 2026-10-21

Approved by Franco on 2026-10-21 ("I approve"). Decision: `wiki/decisions/2026-10-21_decision-design-run-first.md`.

## Goal

Give the web app its own look before M3 and M4 are built. Process as for Skiet Goed: three directions on a Claude Design canvas, Franco picks one, one refinement pass, a Design System artifact, then a Mini design run on its own branch, checked on Franco's phone before merge.

## Rules every direction follows

1. Free resources only: open fonts bundled with the app (no Google Fonts download, so it works offline), open icons, OpenStreetMap data.
2. Phone first at 390 px; must also work on a laptop screen for the control room.
3. Light and dark, both designed. Dark is used at night, not an afterthought.
4. Readable outdoors and at a glance: text meets WCAG AA contrast; status never shown by colour alone.
5. Its own identity, drawn from the content (the map, timelines, the suburbs of Jeffreys Bay), not a template or a look common to AI-built apps.
6. South African formats: dates as 21 Oct 2026, 24-hour time, ZAR.
7. Product words from the vault glossary.

## Screens and states

Sign-in, quick log, full incident form with a privacy guard warning, map, reports feed, settings. States where they apply: normal, empty, loading, offline (with items waiting), error.

## Content and privacy

Made-up demo content only. No real WhatsApp examples. No images of people. No names of private people, phone, ID or registration numbers, house numbers. No SAPS, CPF or security company logos, and nothing that suggests their approval.

## Directions

One of the three is a refinement of the artifact v4 look (teal accent, Barlow, sand and sea map), so the comparison shows whether the fix is polish or something new.

## Checks before Franco chooses

Contrast measured for every text pair, a design critique per direction, an accessibility pass, and this brief's privacy rules.
