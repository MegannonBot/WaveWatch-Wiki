# Button

Buttons do one thing and say it with a verb; each screen has at most one accent button.

- **Primary:** `accent` fill, `on-accent` text, `radius-lg`, at least `touch-target` high (48 to 52px for the main action). "Save incident", "Log incident", "Mark sector D cleared".
- **Outline:** `surface` fill, `ink` text, 1px `control-border`. Cancel, Link, Dismiss, Share brief.
- **Text:** no fill or border, `accent` text, for Change, Full form, More types.
- Focus: `focus-width` ring in `focus-ring` at `focus-offset`.
- Labels in sentence case, `body` size. Never "OK" or "Submit".
