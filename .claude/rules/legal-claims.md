# Rules for legal and regulatory claims

- Primary sources: POPIA (Act 4 of 2013) and the Information Regulator's guidance, the SAPS Act 68 of 1995, the ECT Act 25 of 2002. Quote the section, or mark the claim UNVERIFIED.
- Claims about a provider's region, price or limit (Firebase, Google Cloud, WhatsApp, Anthropic) quote the provider's own current page with the date read, or are UNVERIFIED.
- WaveWatch supports SAPS. Nothing in the app, a report or a pack may suggest WaveWatch investigates, arrests, or acts for SAPS.
- Nothing may imply approval by, or partnership with, SAPS, a CPF, a municipality or a security company unless a signed agreement is on file in `outputs/legal/`.
- Points marked "attorney to confirm" stay open until an attorney's written view is filed.
