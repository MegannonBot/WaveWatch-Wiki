# Jeffreys Bay safety context

## Summary

Background read on 2026-10-20.

## Findings

- 2020 oversight visit: the station was described as under-resourced, with most nights one vehicle and two officers on patrol; 2019 statistics cited at almost one house break-in a day. SAPS disputed the resourcing claim. https://novanews.co.za/kougaexpress/?p=53202 (13 February 2020)
- 2021: four neighbourhood watch groups in Ward 2. https://novanews.co.za/kougaexpress/?p=51023
- July 2026: public violence in Ocean View during a march; spaza shops looted, 69 arrests. https://www.ewn.co.za/2026/07/18/eastern-cape-police-call-for-calm-after-violent-protests-in-kouga

## What it means for us

Patrol targeting and SAPS-ready packs matter. Never record nationality or ethnicity.
