# POPIA and evidence law points

## Summary

Primary and secondary sources read on 2026-10-20. Not legal advice.

## Findings

- SAPS Act s18: CPF objects include partnership, communication, cooperation, service delivery, transparency, and joint problem identification and solving. https://source.acts.co.za/south-african-police-service-act-1995/18__objects_of_community_police_forums_and_boards.php
- POPIA s26 special personal information includes health and criminal behaviour (alleged offences and proceedings); processing is prohibited unless s27 to s33 apply; the Regulator may authorise under s27(2) in the public interest with safeguards. Information Regulator guidance note: https://inforegulator.org.za/wp-content/uploads/2020/07/InfoRegSA-GuidanceNote-Processing-SpecialPersonalInformation-20210628.pdf
- ECT Act s15: data messages are admissible; weight depends on the reliability of generation, storage and integrity, and the identification of the originator (secondary source). https://warwick.ac.uk/fac/soc/law/elj/jilt/2009_1/watney

## What it means for us

Descriptions not identities; minimise health data; record evidence handover precisely.
