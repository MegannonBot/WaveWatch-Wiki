# Comparable community safety platforms

## Summary

Six platforms reviewed on 2026-10-20 for features to borrow.

## Findings

| Platform | Relevant feature | Source |
|---|---|---|
| CrimeSpotter (SA) | Community incidents as map pins and timeline, no personal information on the map | https://stuff.co.za/2020/10/27/drop-everything-and-download-crimespotter/ |
| Community Wolf (SA) | Reporting and location alerts inside WhatsApp; patrol logs; AI on reported data (profile is AI-generated) | https://startup-seeker.com/company/communitywolf~com |
| Namola (SA) | SOS with GPS and callback; community alerts joinable from WhatsApp links | https://support.namola.com/what-is-namola |
| Ushahidi (open source) | Custom forms; map, list and chart views; filters; collections; roles | https://docs.ushahidi.com/platform-user-manual/legacy/overview-of-ushahidi-platform-v3.0 |
| ISS Crime Hub | Precinct crime statistics and maps from SAPS releases | https://crimehub.org/what-is-the-crime-hub |
| Eyes and Ears (BACSA) | Private security reports to a monitoring centre with controllers in SAPS command centres | https://businessreport.co.za/opinion/2025-03-05-business-against-crime-private-security-and-saps-collaborate-in-the-eyes-and-ears-initiative |

## What it means for us

Map plus timeline, WhatsApp-native intake, graded sources and a structured route to SAPS are the patterns to follow.

## Not checked

Pricing and current feature lists of each product.
