# Jeffreys Bay role players from public sources

## Summary

Searched on 2026-10-21 at Franco's request (Needs Franco 5) as a starting list. Organisations only; no private individuals. Background on police resourcing is in [[research/2026-10-20_research_jbay-context]].

## Findings

**Municipal camera network and control room**
- Kouga Municipality runs an Information (or Incident) Command Centre (ICC) at the Kouga fire station in Humansdorp, staffed around the clock, passing information to police and linked to WhatsApp groups and the UrSafe SA app. Reported in April 2024 as 400 cameras, mostly at hotspots, and 35 licence plate recognition (LPR) cameras; in March 2025 as more than 300 municipal and private cameras feeding the ICC. https://novanews.co.za/kougaexpress/kougas-surveillance-system-assists-in-decreasing-crime-20240417/ (18 April 2024); https://theherald.co.za/news/2025-03-25-kouga-sends-in-the-drones-to-fight-crime/ (25 March 2025)
- UrSafe SA is the municipality's private partner (five-year public-private partnership) for the ICC, the UrSAFE Kouga app and drones; a drone team monitors among other places the Jeffreys Bay beachfront. Same sources.
- Drone as a First Responder programme: pilot from 17 March 2025, launched 24 March 2025 in Humansdorp, with UrSafe Technologies under a Memorandum of Agreement (the municipality's page) described by The Herald as a five-year public-private partnership. The ICC sends the nearest drone to an incident; drones carry thermal and live video to the ICC, which coordinates law enforcement and disaster management. Planned to run region-wide around the clock after mid-May 2025. The municipality says a privacy policy applies to drone missions; no detail on retention or access. https://www.kouga.gov.za/kougalocalmunicipalitylaunc ; https://www.dailymaverick.co.za/article/2025-03-24-eastern-capes-kouga-municipality-launches-911-drones-in-first-project-of-its-kind-in-africa/ (24 March 2025)
- Franco, 2026-10-22: the drones do not fly often.
- The Kouga UrSafe app (launched July 2022) is a personal SOS app: an SOS button (free) or a voice safe word (R29.99 a month) sends the user's location and live video and audio to the ICC dashboard and the user's own SOS contacts. ICC operators then contact the right responders (police, security companies, neighbourhood watch, NSRI, ambulance), keep notes, and close the alert when the user is safe. No general incident reporting is described. https://www.kouga.gov.za/kougalaunchesappforimprove (4 July 2022)
- UrSafe's app store listing describes personal safety only (safe words, SOS, location sharing, alerts to chosen contacts, fall and crash detection); no incident logging, case or watch features; last update September 2024. https://apps.apple.com/app/id1481482887 (retrieved 2026-10-22)
- How the public reaches the ICC: residents can set the UrSafe SA app (free and paid versions) to alert the ICC; the ICC is also linked to WhatsApp groups. No published detail of how a call or WhatsApp message becomes a drone dispatch. https://novanews.co.za/kougaexpress/kougas-surveillance-system-assists-in-decreasing-crime-20240417/
- The municipality asks businesses to donate cameras; a local business donated one in 2021. https://www.kouga.gov.za/truetechnologiesgivekougas (20 May 2021)
- A 2025 article on more CCTV cameras in Jeffreys Bay exists but could not be read (site refused access). https://kouganews.com/news/more-cctv-cameras-will-enhance-safety-across-jeffreys-bay-2025-09-08/

**Community structures**
- Wavecrest Watch, founded October 2020 in Wavecrest (the organisation WaveWatch is built for: [[decisions/2026-10-21_decision-built-for-wavecrest-watch]]), patrols and passes information to police and security firms; works with Wavecrest Blockwatch and C-Place Neighbourhood Watch. An earlier Wavecrest Neighbourhood Watch dissolved in 2016. https://novanews.co.za/kougaexpress/?p=51849 (15 October 2020)
- Four neighbourhood watch groups in Ward 2 (2021): see [[research/2026-10-20_research_jbay-context]].
- The municipality's Community Safety Plan lists stakeholders including SAPS, the NSRI, agricultural and civic organisations, emergency services, private security companies, businesses and schools. https://novanews.co.za/kougaexpress/kougas-surveillance-system-assists-in-decreasing-crime-20240417/
- No public source found naming a current Jeffreys Bay Community Police Forum structure.

## What it means for us

- The ICC is the largest camera and LPR network in the area and already feeds police and WhatsApp groups. It is the most important role player to understand before cameras and canvass (M3) are built; it may also overlap with WaveWatch.
- The watches named are candidates for sources and presets once Franco confirms they are active.
