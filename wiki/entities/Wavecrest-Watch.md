# Wavecrest Watch

## Summary

Community watch in Wavecrest, Jeffreys Bay; WaveWatch's first organisation ([[decisions/2026-10-21_decision-built-for-wavecrest-watch]]).

## Key facts

- Founded October 2020; patrols and passes information to police and security firms; works with Wavecrest Blockwatch and C-Place Neighbourhood Watch (Kouga Express, 15 October 2020, [[research/2026-10-21_research_jbay-role-players]]).
- WaveWatch is built for it (Franco, 2026-10-21).

## Relationship to WaveWatch

The organisation the app serves first. WaveWatch is run by Megannon.ME ([[decisions/2026-10-21_decision-operator-megannon-me]]).
