# Glossary

## Definition

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Incident | Something that happened: a crime, collision, emergency or suspicious activity, with a time or time window and a place |
| Report | A raw piece of information as it arrived (message, alert, camera result), graded and linked or dismissed |
| Reports feed | The list of reports waiting to be linked or dismissed |
| Case | An investigation that links incidents, sightings, camera checks and tasks into one pack for SAPS |
| Series | Cases believed to involve the same offenders |
| Subject | An unidentified person or group described by behaviour, clothing and build ("Pair A") |
| Sighting | A time and place a subject was seen, with its source and match confidence |
| Canvass | Asking camera owners to check footage; statuses: not asked, asked, checked and nothing seen, footage received, handed to SAPS |
| Gap street | A street near a case with no checked camera |
| Likely exit route | A street ahead of the last sightings, ranked by direction and distance |
| Operation | A coordinated response: collision, medical, fire, missing person search, water rescue, planned patrol |
| Response log | Who was called, when, and when they arrived, in an operation |
| Sector | An area drawn on the map in a search, assigned and cleared |
| Last known position | Where a missing person was last seen |
| Near-repeat | [[concepts/Near-repeat]] |
| Grade | [[concepts/Source-grading]] |
| Patrol zone | A suggested patrol area with its best time window |
| Case pack | The summary of a case for a SAPS detective |
| After-action report | The summary of an operation without personal details |
| Resident bulletin | The anonymised weekly update for the community group |
| Areas | The 15 suburbs used as working zones ([[decisions/2026-10-20_decision-suburbs-as-zones]]) |

Incident types (22 in 8 groups): break-ins (residential burglary, business burglary, attempted break-in or trespass); vehicle crime (theft from vehicle, vehicle theft or hijacking); violent (robbery or mugging, assault); property (theft other, cable or infrastructure theft, vandalism); suspicious (suspicious activity, LPR or CCTV vehicle alert, begging or harassment at homes); road safety (pedestrian struck, vehicle collision, cyclist or motorcyclist collision); emergencies (medical emergency, fire, missing person, water incident); other (drug-related, other).
