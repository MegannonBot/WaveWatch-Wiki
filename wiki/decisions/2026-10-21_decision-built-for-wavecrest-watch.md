# WaveWatch is built for Wavecrest Watch

## Decision

WaveWatch's first organisation is Wavecrest Watch ([[entities/Wavecrest-Watch]]). Megannon.ME runs the platform ([[decisions/2026-10-21_decision-operator-megannon-me]]).

## Context

Franco, 2026-10-21, on the role-player list: "Wavecrest Watch (founded 2020) is who we are building for".

## Rationale

Franco's ruling.

## Consequences

- The real organisation, when it is created in the beta project, is Wavecrest Watch; demo data keeps the id `demo-jbay`.
- Open: how the working areas relate to Wavecrest (the app covers Jeffreys Bay suburbs, [[decisions/2026-10-20_decision-suburbs-as-zones]]), and whether Hannes coordinates Wavecrest Watch.
- The questions for Hannes in `outputs/legal/2026-10-21_popia-questions-hannes/` should name Wavecrest Watch where they say "the CPF, a neighbourhood watch".
