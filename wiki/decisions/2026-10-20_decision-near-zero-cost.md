# Near-zero running cost

## Decision

Prefer free tiers and no-cost tools. Paid services are added only when a feature needs them, with a budget alert.

## Context

Franco, 2026-10-20: "near zero cost".

## Rationale

An unfunded community project; costs must not depend on a sponsor.

## Consequences

Blaze billing starts only at milestone M6 ([[decisions/2026-10-20_decision-blaze-at-m6]]). Cost estimates are in [[specs/firebase-architecture]].

## Alternatives considered

| Alternative | Why not chosen |
|---|---|
| Managed paid stack from day one | Not justified before use is proven |
