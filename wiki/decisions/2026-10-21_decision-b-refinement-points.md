# Four points from the B refinement approved

## Decision

1. The privacy guard is always on and cannot be switched off in settings.
2. The theme setting offers Light, Dark and Match phone; Match phone is the default.
3. The watch strip always shows the last 24 hours, whatever range the map shows.
4. The list under the map is a sheet that shows one row and pulls up for the rest.

## Context

Proposed by Claude after the refinement of direction B ([[decisions/2026-10-21_decision-design-direction-watch]]), listed in `outputs/product/2026-10-21_design-directions/2026-10-21_product_b-watch-review.md`. Asked "Do you approve all four as proposed, or which would you change?", Franco answered on 2026-10-21: "That looks good."

## Rationale

From the review: the guard is the product's privacy boundary ([[decisions/2026-10-20_decision-identity-boundary]]); Match phone follows the phone's night setting; a fixed 24-hour strip answers "what happened on this watch"; the sheet gives the map back its space.

## Consequences

The Design System artifact for direction B is recorded next, for Franco's approval.

| Alternative | Why not chosen |
|---|---|
