# Email and password plus Google sign-in first; phone sign-in later

## Decision

Milestone M1 uses email and password and Google sign-in. Phone number sign-in is added at milestone M6.

## Context

Approved unchanged by Franco on 2026-10-21 ("Approve").

Phone sign-in by SMS needs the Blaze plan; email link sign-in is limited to 5 emails a day on Spark ([[research/2026-10-20_research_firebase-facts]]).

## Rationale

Keeps the free plan through the coordinator and committee stage.

## Consequences

Invites are by email first.

## Alternatives considered

| Alternative | Why not chosen |
|---|---|
| Phone sign-in from day one | Needs billing |
| Email links | 5 per day on Spark |
