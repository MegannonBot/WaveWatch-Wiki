# First user is the coordinator alone

## Decision

Phase 1 serves one user: Hannes, the coordinator. Other users (committee, street leaders, role players) come later. He mainly uses his phone.

## Context

Franco, 2026-10-20: "It will for now only be for him." and "Device: Phone". The problems named: break-ins, suspicious people in an area, and information living in his head and in WhatsApp groups.

## Rationale

One user keeps the proof of concept cheap and lets the workflow settle before roles and sharing are built.

## Consequences

Every screen is designed phone first. Multi-user roles are proposed for the web app only ([[decisions/2026-10-20_decision-roles]]).

## Alternatives considered

| Alternative | Why not chosen |
|---|---|
| Committee and leaders from the start | Too many unknowns before the workflow is proven |
