# Megannon.ME runs WaveWatch

## Decision

The organisation that runs WaveWatch is Megannon.ME, Franco's company MEGANNONME (Pty) Ltd.

## Context

Asked on 2026-10-21 which organisation runs WaveWatch (the CPF, a neighbourhood watch, a separate body, or MEGANNONME as service provider), Franco answered: "Megannon.ME". The company name is as recorded in the Skiet Goed vault STATE, read 2026-10-21, where the same company runs Skiet Goed.

## Rationale

Franco did not give a reason. The same company already runs Skiet Goed, and the product was kept independent of any one community structure ([[decisions/2026-10-20_decision-product-name-wavewatch]]).

## Consequences

- Whether this makes MEGANNONME (Pty) Ltd the POPIA responsible party for WaveWatch data, and what agreements it needs with the CPF, Hannes and role players, is UNVERIFIED, attorney to confirm (STATE, Needs Franco).
- The SAPS Act s18 basis in [[specs/privacy-and-evidence]] assumed a CPF-run platform; how it applies to a company-run one is UNVERIFIED.
- Organisation-scoped data ([[decisions/2026-10-20_decision-org-scoped-data]]) lets the company serve further communities from one app.
