# Shapes and colours for all eight incident families

## Decision

The five families without a shape now have one, in the design system "WaveWatch Watch" (FamilyShapes and `tokens.json`): robbery and assault, wine-red diamond (`family-violent`); theft and damage, teal hexagon (`family-property`); road safety, olive wide bar (`family-road`); emergencies, plus in ink colour (`family-emergency`); drug-related and other, grey pentagon (`family-other`). Break-ins, suspicious and vehicle keep square, circle and triangle.

## Context

Follows [[decisions/2026-10-21_decision-design-run-choices]]. Franco, 2026-10-21: "Approve".

## Rationale

Every colour measured 4.5:1 or more against the map and surfaces in both themes; the shapes differ so colour is never the only cue.

## Consequences

The vault copy in `outputs/product/2026-10-21_design-system-watch/` was refreshed the same day; the second design run draws them.
