# Three choices from the first design run

## Decision

1. Robbery and assault, theft and damage, road safety, emergencies, and other each get their own shape and colour in the design system, instead of one grey diamond. The exact shapes and colours are proposed in the design system (FamilyShapes) and wait for Franco's yes.
2. The map offers 24 h, 7 d, 30 d, 90 d and All.
3. The newest incident keeps its ring as designed; "Not reported to SAPS" is a filter, not a map marker.

## Context

The first design run ([[reports/2026-10-21_report_design-watch-run]]) listed choices the design system did not settle. Franco chose on 2026-10-21: "Add them to the design", "24h, 7d, 30d, 90d, All" and "As a filter", each the recommended option. Four further choices from that run's handover, section 6, are not yet put to him.

## Rationale

Robbery and fire looked the same on the map; the longer ranges are needed for patterns; the filter already exists.

## Consequences

The second design run builds these, from step 6c.

## Alternatives considered

| Alternative | Why not chosen |
|---|---|
| Keep the grey diamond | Not chosen by Franco |
| Mock-up ranges only, or the artifact's five | Not chosen by Franco |
| A second marker for unreported incidents | Not chosen by Franco |
