# The product is called WaveWatch

## Decision

The product is named WaveWatch everywhere: the app, documents, reports and file names.

## Context

Franco, 2026-10-20: "The name should be WaveWatch, not CPF."

## Rationale

Keeps the product independent of any one structure; which organisation runs it was ruled on 2026-10-21 ([[decisions/2026-10-21_decision-operator-megannon-me]]).

## Consequences

The artifact, spec and guide were renamed on 2026-10-20. The backup format keeps the id `jbay-cpf-desk` as an accepted older value.

## Alternatives considered

| Alternative | Why not chosen |
|---|---|
| J-Bay CPF Desk | Rejected by Franco |
