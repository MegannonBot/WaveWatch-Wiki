# Suburbs are the working areas

## Decision

Incidents, filters, patterns and patrol zones use suburbs. SAPS sectors may be added later.

## Context

Franco, 2026-10-20: "Suburbs for now".

## Rationale

Suburbs are how residents and the WhatsApp groups describe places.

## Consequences

Fifteen areas are configured, four with estimated centres to be set on the map (Supertubes, Fountains / Industrial, Ocean View, Tokyo Sexwale).
