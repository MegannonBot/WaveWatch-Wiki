# The user guide is a PDF

## Decision

User guides for WaveWatch are delivered as PDF files.

## Context

Franco, 2026-10-20: "It must be a PDF", while an editable doc was being started.

## Rationale

A PDF can be sent to the coordinator on WhatsApp and read offline.

## Consequences

The 8-page guide is in `outputs/product/2026-10-20_user-guide/`. A part-written editable doc "WaveWatch User Guide" remains in Franco's Claude files.

## Alternatives considered

| Alternative | Why not chosen |
|---|---|
| Editable doc | Not what Franco asked for |
